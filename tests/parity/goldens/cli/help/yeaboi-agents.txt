usage: yeaboi agents [-h] {cost,advisor,standup,security} ...

The Agents modes are in beta — costs and activity are estimates from local
session logs and public rate tables, not your provider's bill. Treat every
number as an estimate to verify, not an invoice.

positional arguments:
  {cost,advisor,standup,security}
    cost                What your agents cost: per-model/project/source
                        breakdowns + daily trend
    advisor             How much of your agent spend is recoverable: Read
                        waste, cache health, prompt-prefix churn
    standup             Daily digest of what your agents did (sessions +
                        agent-authored commits/PRs)
    security            Audit your agent setup: permissions, MCP servers,
                        secrets exposure, risky commands

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
